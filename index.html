ABOUT

Hello I’m Anthony Varas, a Florida born artist and inventor exploring the art and science of ceramics. I built a custom camera rig to capture the way ceramic glazes flow, collide and transform, turning the creative process into part of the artwork.

I sell the finished pieces created through these experiments alongside other functional ceramic work. My goal is to create art that is visual and useful; something you can watch, collect, display and use.

I’m continuing to develop more immersive flow art videos designed to mesmerize viewers while bringing that same sense of movement, color and experimentation into my functional artwork.

Follow me on social media to watch my artistic journey!
